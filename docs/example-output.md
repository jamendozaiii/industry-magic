# Worked Example (anonymized)

> All company names, account IDs, and numbers below are **fictional** and for illustration only.

## Scenario

An account team is preparing a Q2 2026 EBR for a wealth-management customer and wants to show how the customer compares to its industry and two peers, including quarter-over-quarter change.

**Parameters set on the Operations tab:**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Industry | Wealth |
| Year | 2026 |
| Quarter | Q2 |
| Comparison Type | Quarterly |
| Main Org | Acme Financial (0001) |
| Company A | Northwind Mutual (0002) |
| Company B | Cascade Advisors (0003) |

## Output — "Report - Change Incl" tab

Values shown with quarter-over-quarter change in parentheses.

**Date Range: Q2, 2026**

| Strengthening Presence | Acme Financial | Industry Average | Northwind Mutual | Cascade Advisors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workspaces | 82 (+3) | 78 | 105 (−1) | 106 |
| Social Accounts per workspace | 1.00 (0) | 1.00 | 1.00 (0) | 1.00 |
| Connections per workspace | 1,600 (+90) | 1,480 | 1,590 (+15) | 1,570 |
| Average LinkedIn Connections | 1,600 (+90) | 1,480 | 1,585 (+15) | 1,565 |

| Publishes and Engagements | Acme Financial | Industry Average | Northwind Mutual | Cascade Advisors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quarterly Publishes per workspace | 18 (+1) | 16 | 12 (0) | 11 |
| % of original publishes per workspace | 8% (+1%) | 6% | 19% (+9%) | 10% |
| Overall engagement rate | 5.10 (+0.80) | 4.15 | 8.40 (−0.10) | 8.55 |
| LinkedIn engagement rate | 5.10 (+0.85) | 4.14 | 8.39 (−0.11) | 8.55 |

## Output — "Report - No Change" tab

Same selection, current values only (no deltas). This is the version used when the slide shouldn't emphasize movement over time.

## Reading the table

- **Acme Financial** column is the customer under review.
- **Industry Average** is the Wealth-sector benchmark for the period.
- **Northwind Mutual** and **Cascade Advisors** are the two peers chosen for context.
- In the "Change Incl" version, `(+n)` / `(−n)` shows movement vs. Q1 2026 (because Comparison Type was Quarterly). A Yearly comparison would instead compare against Q2 2025.
